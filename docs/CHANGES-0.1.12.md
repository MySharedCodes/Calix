# Calix 0.1.12

- Only complete authored case notebooks use output labels. Basic demonstrations, including numeric pools, units, statistics, fitting, symbolic solving and plotting, return their ordinary results. Plot legends remain where they identify curves.
- The analytical section is now Analytical and symbolic calculations, ahead of Statistics. Five new demonstration entries provide factorization, rational simplification, series, trigonometric expansions and complex simplification, with three variants each.
- TrigExpand uses SymPy's trigonometric expansion. ComplexExpand accepts one scalar expression and assumes all symbolic variables are real, matching the standard Wolfram-language convention. It uses temporary real symbols without changing the notebook's stored definitions. Optional complex-variable lists are not implemented.
- The coil notebook now integrates six independent equal-mass particles: three positive and three negative, all with different initial positions and velocities. The first particle retains its analytic uniform-field comparison. Speed-squared drift checks cover all six trajectories.
- Magnetic scenes now contain 48 field lines, compared with twelve previously. Field lines use thin translucent gray-teal strokes; six saturated trajectories use 2.6 CSS-pixel strokes via the existing Three.js line addons. Every particle has a distinct color and charge legend. MagneticFieldPlot3D accepts two to six paths and an optional list of charges, in units of e, before the final time interval. Omitted charges produce generic particle labels.
- Magnetic scene coordinates are rounded to six decimal places in units of coil radius, with 121 samples per field-line half of length 9R. This bounds coordinate rounding by 0.0000005R and reduces workspace storage. Numerical solutions, energy checks and the field texture retain their original calculation precision.
- Added IMO 1981 Problem 3: an exact finite search maximizes m^3+n^3 for integers m,n in 1...1981 with (n^2-mn-m^2)^2=1. It solves the algebraic branches, examines 3,962 candidates and checks the original constraint. The maximum is 5,034,507,976 at (987,1597).
- Added IPhO 2011 theoretical Problem 3, the final theoretical problem: ion scattering by an induced dipole. The notebook calculates the dipole-field limit, force, interaction potential, turning points, critical impact parameter and capture cross section, then plots dimensionless effective potentials and the scattering branch. The atom is fixed, motion is nonrelativistic and radiation is neglected. The equality case is identified as critical rather than an ordinary scattering trajectory.

Competition statements were checked against the question PDFs. The calculation cells were authored for Calix. No solution text or source PDFs are included in the application. No dependencies were added; distribution retains the existing notices and user notebooks.
