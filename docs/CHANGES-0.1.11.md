# Calix 0.1.11

- The air resistance notebook now has two parts: linear drag with analytic velocities and integrated positions, followed by quadratic drag with numerical velocities, integrated positions, trajectory animation and comparison plots. Parameters are m=1 kg, g=9.81 m/s^2, initial velocity (10,15) m/s, linear coefficient b=0.2 kg/s and quadratic coefficient c=0.02 kg/m.
- The charged-particle notebook uses three equal-current coaxial loops at z/R=-0.7,0,0.7. The magnetic field is calculated from elliptic-integral expressions. Two independent particles have charges +e and -e with the same proton mass, position and velocity; mutual interactions and radiation are omitted. Their trajectories use the native C++/Boost numerical integrator. The uniform-center-field approximation retains its analytic solution.
- MagneticFieldPlot3D combines coil geometry, both trajectories, twelve field lines and two translucent axial slices, without arrows. Field lines integrate dr/ds=+/-B/|B| using the native solver. Slices use a 41^3 grid and log(1+|B|/B0) color mapping, clipped at |B|/B0=3. The 0.075R neighborhood of each ideal wire is excluded from the sampled display. B0 is the combined central field; on-axis samples use the analytic limit. The opacity control changes the slice visibility independently of the color scale.
- The many-body notebook includes planar figure-eight motion, a planar four-body system and a spatial five-body system. NBodySolve[masses,positions,velocities,{t,lower,upper}] supports two to five bodies in 2D or 3D, with G=1. NBodyPlot3D[positionFunctions,masses,{t,lower,upper}] displays paths, moving bodies and a potential volume with a fixed color scale over time.
- The volume shows gravitational potential per unit test mass, Phi=U/m_test. Rendering uses -Sum[m_i/sqrt(r_i^2+epsilon^2)] with epsilon=0.12, solely to display finite colors near point masses. The equations of motion and total-energy checks use unsoftened Newtonian gravity. Collisions and close approaches can limit the usable integration interval. Energy and momentum checks are included in the spatial chapter.
- Statistics now includes three fitting variants: linear least squares with R^2 and RMSE, quadratic least squares, and exponential decay fitted by least squares of log(y). The exponential fit minimizes errors in log space; its displayed residuals and RMSE use the original y scale. All variants overlay observations and model curves and show residual plots. Show combines two to ten 2D plots, with a maximum of twenty traces.
- Field scenes support rotation, bounded zoom, playback, scrubbing, opacity, PNG export and saved-notebook restoration. New functions are available in editor completion.
- The service socket begins listening before emitting its startup handshake, so an immediate desktop connection can queue while Uvicorn starts accepting requests.

No dependencies were added. Closed-source distribution continues to include the existing third-party notices. Existing user documents are preserved; revised built-in notebooks have a new version key.
